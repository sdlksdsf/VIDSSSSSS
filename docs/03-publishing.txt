==================== TITLE ====================

The Kid Who Could Stop Time

Why: the three biggest reference videos use the same formula, a plain statement of the power with no payoff revealed:
- The Kid Who Could Phase Through Anything (21M views)
- He Could See One Word Above Everyone's Head (16M views)
- Everything He Touched Turned to Gold (9.7M views)
This title follows that formula, matches the narrator's first sentence, and does not give away the twist.

Alternatives to test:
1. He Could Stop Time With One Snap
2. Every Time He Snapped, The World Froze
3. He Stopped Time Whenever He Wanted

Avoid titles like "...But It Cost Him Everything" or "...Until He Looked In The Mirror". They give away the ending.
