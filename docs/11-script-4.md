# Script 4 - "Only One Country Is In All 4 Hemispheres"

Status: awaiting approval. About 125 words, 40-45 seconds, one narrator, map animation.

| # | Line | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Only one country on Earth sits in all four hemispheres. | Globe spins, equator and the 180 line light up, four quarters labelled N S E W, a "?" pin. |
| 2 | It's not Russia. It's not the United States. | Each lights up red, big X stamp. |
| 3 | Most people can't even find it on a map. | Camera flies over the empty Pacific, searching. |
| 4 | It's thirty-three tiny islands, scattered across a patch of ocean bigger than India. | Dots pop up one by one, outline of India dropped on top for scale. |
| 5 | Put all its land together, and it's about the size of New York City. | Islands slide together into one small blob next to NYC. |
| 6 | The date line used to cut right through it. So half the country was living in yesterday. | Date line splits the islands, "MONDAY" / "SUNDAY" on each side. |
| 7 | So in 1995, it just moved the line. | The line bends east around the islands. |
| 8 | Now it's the first place on Earth to see every new day. | Sunrise sweeps in, clock reads UTC+14, "FIRST". |
| 9 | But most of it is less than two metres above the sea. It could be one of the first countries to disappear. | Side view: thin strip of land, water level rising, 2 m ruler. |
| 10 | The country is Kiribati. | Name and flag slam in. |
| 11 | And no, you don't say it like that. It's "Kiribas". | "KIRIBATI" crossed out, "KI-RI-BAS" in its place. Cut. |

Facts used: Kiribati lies in all four hemispheres; 33 islands; sea area about 3.4 million km2 (India 3.29 million); land 811 km2 (New York City land about 780); date line moved 1 Jan 1995; Line Islands are UTC+14; most land under 2 m; "ti" is pronounced "s".
