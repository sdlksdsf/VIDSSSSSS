# Script v1 — "The Kid Who Could Stop Time"

**Status: awaiting your approval. No animation has been produced yet.**

- Format: vertical 9:16, 55–60 s, third-person narrator, burned-in word-by-word captions
- Title for upload: `The Kid Who Could Stop Time #roblox #viral #shorts`
- Structure: the "Ever since ___ was a kid" formula used by 3 of your 5 reference videos (see `01-analysis.md`)

## Narration (this is exactly what the voice-over says)

> Ever since Eli was a kid, he could stop time.
>
> One snap of his fingers, and the whole world froze. Birds hung in the air. Raindrops stopped falling. Even his mom froze mid-sentence.
>
> At first, he thought it was the best power in the world. He finished every test. He dodged every dodgeball. He always got the last slice of pizza.
>
> As he got older, he used it for everything. An extra hour of sleep. An extra day to study. Sometimes he stayed in the frozen world for weeks, just because it was quiet.
>
> Then one morning, at his graduation, his best friend walked up to him and stopped smiling. He just stared.
>
> "Eli... what happened to you?"
>
> Eli laughed it off. But his hands were shaking. His stomach dropped.
>
> He ran inside and looked in the mirror. And he froze.
>
> Gray hair. Tired eyes. A face he didn't recognize.
>
> Every time he snapped, the world stopped. But he never did.
>
> Eli hadn't been stopping time. He'd been spending his.

## Why each part is there

| Technique seen in your references | Where it is in this script |
|---|---|
| Name and impossible premise in the first sentence | Shot 1 |
| Three concrete examples right after the hook | Shot 2 (birds, rain, mom) and shot 3 (test, dodgeball, pizza) |
| "At first" fun phase, then "As he got older" time skip | Shots 3 and 4 |
| "Then one morning" pivot around the 40–55% mark | Shot 5, at 55% of the word count |
| Very short sentences once tension starts | Shots 7–9 |
| Information withheld visually | Eli's face is hidden from shot 5 until the reveal |
| Last sentence flips the meaning of the first | "He hadn't been stopping time. He'd been spending his." |
| Ends on the twist with no outro | Hard cut on the final word, which makes it loop back into shot 1 |
| Clue planted early for re-watchers | The grey streak in shot 4 |
