# Script 2 — "In This Town Nobody Turns Off the Lights"

Status: awaiting approval. Target 55–60 seconds, 9:16, about 180 words at a faster read.
Voices: narrator (the kid, first person), the Mayor, and one line shouted by the kid.

## Narration

1. In this town, nobody has turned off a light in a hundred years.
2. Kids sleep in sunglasses. Dogs wear flashlights. Even the fridge has a night-light.
3. They say the Dark is alive. And whoever it touches… disappears.
4. We moved in on Sunday. Nobody told me the rule.
5. That night, I flipped my switch. *Click.*
6. A man screamed. A lady fainted. Someone threw a lamp through my window.
7. At school they called me Lights Out.
8. But something felt off. Everyone here looked so… tired.
9. On Thursday, Old Pete's bulb burned out. A black shape slid under his door. By morning, Pete was gone.
10. The mayor pointed at me. **MAYOR:** "You woke it up. Tonight, you sit in the dark. Alone."
11. They left me in the square and switched off the last lamp.
12. Two huge eyes opened above me. A giant hand came down…
13. …and tucked a blanket around me.
14. The Dark didn't take people. It put them to bed.
15. Nobody here had slept in a hundred years.
16. Behind it, Old Pete was snoring.
17. **KID (shouting):** "It's not a monster. It's bedtime!"
18. One by one, the lights went out.
19. And the mayor? He owned the light bulb factory.

Optional last line, in the style of the samples: "Like and subscribe, or I'm leaving your light on."

## How it uses the strategy

- Rule and stakes in sentence 1; gag run in seconds 2–9; legend; outsider breaks the rule; three reactions ending on the silliest; nickname; planted clue ("tired"); day of the week and a named victim; mayor's ultimatum in a second voice; alone at night; reframe ("didn't take people, put them to bed"); payoff run; mayor punchline.
- The first frame is the brightest image in the film, and the story moves from all-light to all-dark, so colour and lighting are the story.
- Final "click" to black matches the "Click" in line 5, which makes the loop clean.
