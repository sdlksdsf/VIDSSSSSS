# Script 2 — "In This Town Nobody Turns Off the Lights"

Status: approved with changes (stronger hook, joke ending). Final video: `render/nobody-turns-off-the-lights.mp4`, 59.96 s, 9:16.
Voices: narrator (the kid, first person), the Mayor, and one line shouted by the kid.

## Narration (final, as recorded)

Hook (0–4 s): the picture shows a finger, a switch, CLICK, darkness, then every head in the street turning, the whole town screaming and a lamp smashing into the lens.

1. I turned off my bedroom light… and the whole town screamed.
2. Because here, nobody has turned off a light in a hundred years.
3. Kids sleep in sunglasses. Dogs wear flashlights. Even the fridge has a night-light.
4. They say the Dark is alive. And whoever it touches… disappears.
5. We'd moved in that morning. Nobody told me the rule.
6. At school, they called me Lights Out.
7. But something felt off. Everyone here looked so tired.
8. On Thursday, Old Pete's bulb burned out. A black shape slid under his door. By morning, Pete was gone.
9. **MAYOR:** "You woke it up! Tonight, you sit in the dark. Alone."
10. They left me in the square, and switched off the last lamp.
11. Two huge eyes opened above me. A giant hand came down…
12. …and tucked me in.
13. The Dark didn't take people. It put them to bed.
14. Nobody here had slept in a hundred years.
15. So one by one, the lights went out.
16. And the mayor? He owned the light bulb factory.
17. So the Dark tucked him in first.
18. **MAYOR:** "But I'm not even tired!" (falls asleep instantly, snores; last light clicks off)

No "like and subscribe", no "watch till the end", no "thanks for watching". The ending joke is the payoff.

## How it uses the strategy

- Rule and stakes in sentence 1; gag run in seconds 2–9; legend; outsider breaks the rule; three reactions ending on the silliest; nickname; planted clue ("tired"); day of the week and a named victim; mayor's ultimatum in a second voice; alone at night; reframe ("didn't take people, put them to bed"); payoff run; mayor punchline.
- The first frame is the brightest image in the film, and the story moves from all-light to all-dark, so colour and lighting are the story.
- Final "click" to black matches the "Click" in line 5, which makes the loop clean.
