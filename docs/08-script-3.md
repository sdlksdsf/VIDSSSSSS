## Why this one should hold people

- **0–2 s:** a wallet, a hand, a grab, "THIEF!" The viewer sees a kid get blamed for doing the right thing before they can swipe.
- **Question planted at 2 s:** does the liar get away with it? Second question at line 5: if the wallet is empty, why does he want it so badly?
- **Payoff rhythm:** fries coupon (line 5), "not a magician" (8), fake crying (9), "Kevin" (12), the ID (13), "old photo" (14), Margaret (15), his confession (16), the cop (17), the fries (18). Nothing longer than 6 seconds without one.
- **Clue shown early:** the wallet is opened on screen at line 5, so the ID at line 11 is fair.
- **Ending:** the villain convicts himself, the authority figure gets the last joke, and the fries coupon from line 5 pays off in the final shot.
