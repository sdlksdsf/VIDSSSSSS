## Final script (≈205 words, ≈55–62 s)

> In this town, when you forget someone's name, their face goes blurry. And they can't
> hear you until you say it again.
>
> Last week, Jake forgot the baker's name. Now he buys bread from a smudge.
>
> Me? I never forget a name. I remember everyone at school. Everyone… except my best
> friend. Eight years I've called him "buddy."
>
> Then the school roof caved in. The fire chief said they couldn't dig — too much
> rubble. But I knew he was under there. And a blur can't hear you.
>
> "Buddy! Dude! Best friend!" Nothing. Gary from my class laughed. "You forgot your
> best friend's name? That's brutal."
>
> I didn't care. I climbed onto the rubble, and then I remembered the first day we met.
> He pushed half his sandwich at me and said, "I'm Dex. What's yours?"
>
> "Dex!" I screamed. His face snapped back, clear as day. He looked up through the dust,
> grinned, and said, "I knew you'd remember."
>
> Now I've said his name a thousand times. He's hiding from me. His face is fine. Mine
> isn't.

## Rule compliance (from the round-6 research)

- Hook = the town rule in sentence 1, on screen from frame one (the 9M+ sample structure).
- Instant consequence by second 5–12: "buys bread from a smudge."
- Question planted by second 10, answered only in the last 5 seconds.
- Joke/reversal every ~8 s: smudge gag, Gary's "that's brutal", sandwich memory,
  the hiding punchline.
- Emotional core: the first day they met, "I knew you'd remember."
- Hard cut on the punchline. No CTA, no "watch till the end", no "stop scrolling",
  no subscribe ask.
- Several voices: narrator (Red), Dex, Gary. Word-by-word captions colour-coded.

## REMAKE FIXES (vs. broken v1)

1. **Voice:** brand-new lighter/younger narrator voice (v1's voice-09 was too deep);
   fresh Dex and Gary voices too.
2. **Captions:** rebuilt — every line shows its full sentence with word-by-word
   highlight timed proportionally inside the line's own start/end (v1's pocketsphinx
   word timings drifted, dropping/mis-syncing captions). Dark readability bar added.
3. **Effects:** flashes, shakes and overlays reduced and smoothed; post FX
   (saturation/bloom/vignette) eased so nothing flickers or overwhelms.
4. **Framing:** camera pull increased and targets re-centred so narrator and scene
   details are never cut off at 9:16 edges.
5. **Narration integrity:** clip edges padded so no word is clipped.
