## Story (approved direction)

Red is the kid who never forgets a name — except he has never said his best friend's real
name out loud, he just calls him "buddy". When the school roof caves in and his friend is
trapped under the rubble, he can't reach him: a person whose name you've forgotten can't
hear you. He has to dig through the wreckage and remember the one name he never used.

## Final script (≈215 words, ≈55–60 s)

> In this town, when you forget someone's name, their face goes blurry. And they can't
> hear you until you say it again.
>
> Last week, Jake forgot the baker's name. Now he buys bread from a smudge.
>
> Me? I never forget a name. I remember everyone at school. Everyone… except my best
> friend. I've called him "buddy" for eight years. I never said his real name out loud.
> So when I looked at him, I just saw a blur.
>
> Then the school roof caved in. The fire chief said they couldn't dig — too much
> rubble. But I knew he was under there. And a blur can't hear you.
>
> "Buddy! Dude! Best friend!" Nothing. Gary from my class laughed. "You forgot your best
> friend's name? That's brutal."
>
> I didn't care. I climbed onto the rubble, and then I remembered the first day we met.
> He pushed half his sandwich toward me and said, "I'm Dex. What's yours?"
>
> "Dex!" I screamed.
>
> His face snapped back, clear as day. He looked up through the dust and grinned. "I knew
> you'd remember."
>
> Now I've said his name a thousand times. He's starting to hide from me. His face is
> fine. Mine isn't.

## Why this works (mapped to the rules)

- **Hook (0–5 s):** the impossible town rule stated in sentence 1, shown on screen from
  frame one — the exact structure of the 9M/9.6M samples.
- **Instant consequence (5–12 s):** "buys bread from a smudge" — one visual gag.
- **Question planted by second 10:** *what happens to the one name he never said?* Only
  the ending answers it.
- **Joke/reversal every ~8 s:** smudge gag, Gary's "that's brutal", the sandwich memory,
  the hiding punchline.
- **Emotional core:** the first day they met; the line "I knew you'd remember."
- **Hard cut on a punchline:** "His face is fine. Mine isn't." — rewards watching to the
  end. No CTA, no "watch till the end", no "stop scrolling", no subscribe ask.
- **Several voices:** narrator (Red, male, hoodie kid), fire chief, Gary, Dex.
- **Educational-fun angle for the new channel:** it's a story, but the rule is a playful
  lesson about attention and remembering people — fits "fun educational, makes you smarter".
