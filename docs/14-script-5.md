# Short 5 - "How A Bruise Changes Colour 😯"

Status: approved (simpler rewrite below), produced. 23 seconds. Narrator voice-08.

## Final approved script
You know when you walk into a table and smack your shin? The skin doesn't break. But under it, tiny blood vessels pop. The blood has nowhere to go, so it just sits there. That's the dark mark you see. After a day or two, it turns blue and purple. Then your body sends in cells to clean it up. They eat the old blood, and it goes green, then yellow. Two weeks later, it's gone. Like it never happened.

## Why this topic
- Same family as the two biggest samples (stitches 255M, splinters 216M): a body event everybody has had, told to "you".
- The colour change is something the viewer has seen but never had explained, and it is very visual.
- The hook is in second one: the hit on the shin.

## Facts
- A bruise is blood from broken capillaries trapped under unbroken skin.
- Red -> blue/purple as haemoglobin loses oxygen (1-2 days).
- White blood cells (macrophages) break haemoglobin down: biliverdin is green, bilirubin is yellow (about days 5-10).
- Most bruises clear in about two weeks.
